# Onboarding evidence: published tests

Collected 2026-10-29. Every number was read on the page it comes from. Where abtest.design summarises another source, the original was opened too when reachable, and both URLs are listed. Cite tests by their number (#N) in the teardown. Numbers are relative lifts unless they are marked as absolute rates or percentage points (pp).

**Evidence strength:** strong = controlled test (A/B, split or controlled experiment) with numbers; directional = numbers without a stated method, or a confounded or cumulative result; anecdotal = claim without numbers.

**Bucket** uses the RevenueCat SOSA taxonomy: Health & Fitness, Education, Productivity, Utilities, Photo & Video, Media & Entertainment, Social & Lifestyle, Business. "n/a" marks web e-commerce or SaaS that sits outside the app taxonomy.
