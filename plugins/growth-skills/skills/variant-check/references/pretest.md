# Clarity pretest: questions per mechanic

A short unmoderated test before live traffic: show the variant for a few
seconds (or let people look freely), then ask. Tools: Lyssna
(https://www.lyssna.com) five-second and first-click tests, Maze
(https://maze.co), or the team's own panel. Show A and B to different people.

The pretest checks understanding, not preference. "Which do you like more?"
predicts little; "how much will you pay today?" catches a real problem.

Always ask (every paywall):
1. "How much would you pay today if you tapped the main button?"
   Pass: the amount shown for today (often $0 with a trial). If people can't
   read the terms at all, that's the finding: the terms are too small.
2. "What happens after that, and when?" Pass: the price and the day of the
   first charge, if there is a trial.
3. "What do you get if you pay?" Pass: names at least one real benefit shown.
4. "How would you leave this screen without paying?" Pass: finds the ✕ or
   "not now".

Add per mechanic in the variant:

| Mechanic | Question | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Trial timeline | "When would you get a reminder?" (only if B promises one) | the reminder day shown |
| 2 Annual as hero | "Which plan is the best deal, and why?" | annual, with the saving or per-month price |
| 3 Trial on annual only | "Can you try any plan for free? Which?" | annual only |
| 4 Social proof | "Do other people use this? How do you know?" | cites the rating or review |
| 5 Comparison | "What can you do on the paid plan that you can't for free?" | one row from the table |
| 6 Prices up front | "How much is the cheapest option?" | the right price, without tapping |
| 7 Multi-step | "Before you saw the price, what were you told you'd get?" | a benefit from step 1 |
| 8 Button | "What happens when you tap the main button?" | matches the button's promise |
| 9 Delayed close | first click: "Show how you'd close this" | finds ✕ after it appears; note confusion |
| 10 Visuals | "What does this app do for you?" | the product's job, from the visual |
| 11 Price level | (no clarity question; price tests need live traffic) | — |
| 12 Copy | "In your own words, what's the main promise here?" | the new headline's idea |
| 13 Trial added | "Is there a free period? How long, and what then?" | the trial length and the price after it |

How to read it:
- Look at wrong answers, not averages. If a few people misread what they'll
  pay today, fix it before launch: that misread also hurts trust and store
  review.
- A pretest can't predict the lift. It removes reasons a good idea would
  lose for avoidable reasons.
- Don't run it on people who designed the screen.
