## Notes by bucket

- **Health & Fitness** and **Education** have the highest "not enough usage"
  share (40.4% and 39.5%, B36) and the clearest daily habit. Streak and
  reminder patterns fit. Most published streak evidence is Duolingo
  (Education). Its strong tests (#R25, #R28, #R29) keep their High priority
  in any bucket with a daily habit; say in the row that the source is
  Duolingo.
- **Utilities** and **Photo & Video** often have no daily habit. Score
  Dimension 3 as "no habit loop" where that's true, and put the weight on
  the welcome, the first result and the trial-ending notice.
- **AI apps** (tag): annual 12-month retention 21.1% vs 30.7% for non-AI
  (B24), and trial → paid 20.60% vs 25.59% across all apps on the Adapty
  panel (B14). Showing the user what they made (pattern 17) matters more
  when the output is the value.
- **Annual plans in any bucket**: 34.2% of annual cancellations come in
  month 1 (B26); a recap that waits for month 11 arrives late.
