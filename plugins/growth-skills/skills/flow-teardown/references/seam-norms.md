# Seam norms by bucket

What top apps in each bucket do at the seam, from public teardowns (Mobbin iOS
flows and published case studies, October 2026). Norms describe common
practice, not what wins. Buckets follow RevenueCat's State of Subscription
Apps categories, the same taxonomy as the paywall and onboarding skills; "AI
app" is a tag, not a bucket. For the full category notes, see the
onboarding-teardown skill's `categories.md`.

| Bucket | Flow shape | First paywall | Trial | After close (observed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health & Fitness: diet, workouts, cycle | Long quiz funnel | End of flow, after a numeric plan | 3 days (AI) to 1 month | One-time deep discount (Cal AI 80%, Flo 44%); Fitbod meters workouts instead |
| Health & Fitness: meditation, sleep | Short state quiz | After a recommended course | 7–14 days, very transparent | Free tier or monthly offer; some leaders have no onboarding paywall |
| Education: language, brain | Lesson-first freemium | Soft, in-feed or contextual | 7 days | Straight into the free product |
| Education: books, self-growth | Long quiz funnel | End of flow | 7 days with timeline | Monthly as the second offer (Headway) |
| Productivity: tools (todo, notes) | Account then product | None at first run | — | — |
| Productivity: behaviour change (focus, habits, planners) | Quiz funnel | End of flow, after a report | 3–7 days | Monthly downsell (Opal), referral offer, second paywall after the walkthrough |
| Productivity: AI assistants | Auth, age, chat | None (big assistants) or soft after sign-up | None or ≤3 days | Upgrade pill and limit-hit paywalls |
| Utilities: VPN, password | Account, then hard paywall | Within the first ~12 screens | None (intro discount) or 14 days | Product doesn't work without a plan |

## Reading the table

- **No onboarding paywall** (big AI assistants, dating, tool-first productivity,
  some meditation and workout apps) is a model, not a gap: score the seam at the
  first limit-hit paywall.
- **Early hard gate** (VPN, password managers, cleaners) is the norm where the
  product has no free use; judge continuity and trial clarity, not position.
- **Value-first seams** (finance own-data reveal, Photo & Video first result,
  diet plan reveal) set the bar for "right after the value moment".
- AI apps start more trials (download-to-trial median 8.5% vs 5.6%) but retain
  worse; an onboarding that oversells capability shows up as refunds after the
  seam, not at it.
