# In-product upsells on Mobbin: field notes

Collected 2026-10-29 with the Mobbin MCP (`search_screens`, `search_flows`). iOS first, plus 9 web screens. Every description is our own paraphrase of what the screen shows. No Mobbin text is quoted beyond a few words of a button label, and no images were downloaded or stored. Open the `mobbin_url` to see the screen.

**Tier:** everything here is **observed** (seen in shipping apps, no public test attached). Nothing in this file is evidence that a pattern converts.

**Caveats**
- Mobbin shows single screens or short flows. When only one screen exists, the **trigger is inferred** from the screen state and marked "(inferred)".
- Frequency (how often a prompt reappears) is almost never visible on Mobbin. "Frequency hints" lists only what the screen itself shows, such as a reset timer, a renewal date or an "ends in" countdown.
- Buckets are RevenueCat SOSA buckets based on primary App Store category. "AI" is a tag, not a bucket. Finance apps fall outside the eight target buckets and are labelled "Finance (out of scope)". We kept them because the pattern is clean.

## 2. Patterns seen

Counts are unique apps across the table and the extra list (iOS + web). One app can show several patterns.

1. **Visible usage counter or meter before the wall (17 apps).** Remaining uses appear as a bar, ring, "x of y" or a balance. Usually it sits next to the action (Canva under the generate button) or in a usage sheet (Photoroom, Ahead, Bevel, Mimo, Raycast, Recime, Google One, GoPro Quik, Duolingo hearts/energy, Tinder Boosts, Replika, Notion, Fuse). Web: Klaviyo, Rox, Synthesia. Almost always paired with a reset time or renewal date, which tells the user that waiting is an option.
2. **Contextual feature gate that names the blocked task (16 apps).** Tapping a locked feature opens a sheet or modal whose headline is that feature, not generic Premium copy: Duolingo explain-my-answer, Photoroom video generator, Apple Invites "upgrade to invite", Todoist permissions, Coffee Meets Bagel notes, Replika, Insight Timer course, Universe commerce, Notion wiki tour, Stake. Web: Figma Dev Mode, Gamma, Bonsai, Circle, Mixpanel, Savee. Most show 3–4 benefit bullets and one CTA. Price on the CTA shows up mainly on web and in Apple's own apps.
3. **Upgrade offers to existing subscribers: higher tier, family, annual (16 apps).** Duolingo (Max, Family, manage-plan stack), Azar (Plus→Premium, current tier named), MyFitnessPal (Premium→Premium+), WHOOP (upgrade and downgrade side by side), Vivid (price lock), Coinbase and GoPro Quik (monthly→annual), YouTube, Apple Music, Calm, Cal AI, Waking Up, Truecaller (family), Strava (bundle with a partner app), ChatGPT (Go vs Plus), X (Basic→higher). Family offers lead with the per-month extra, not the full price.
4. **Always-on upsell surfaces: settings card, nav tab, home banner, profile hub (15 apps).** A dedicated Premium/Upgrade tab (Spotify, Truecaller, Universe, Monzo during its trial), a hero card at the top of Settings (Perplexity, Raycast, Opal, Mammoth, Ladder, Tinder, Strava), a crown row (MyFitnessPal), a home-feed banner (Suno), a stats footer (Neuecast) or a profile hub (Yubo, X Premium hub). None of these can be dismissed.
5. **Consumables or credit packs next to, or instead of, the subscription (14 apps).** AI credit packs (CapCut, Bevel, Riveo, Ahead, Mimo, Perplexity, Anything, Emergent web) and social/game consumables (Tinder Top Picks/Boosts, Coffee Meets Bagel beans, Yubo, Duolingo gems, Forest minute bundles, Replika). Packs use volume tiers with bonus credits or strikethroughs and per-unit pricing. Credits often roll over or "never expire", which is stated on the sheet.
6. **A non-paying way out of the wall (12 apps).** Wait for reset with a timer (Duolingo, Lex, Recime with a "remind me" link, Ahead, X, Krea), watch an ad (Duolingo energy, Forest), earn by doing (Duolingo practice, Tinder challenge Boost), invite friends (Riveo), clean up (Google One), use the remaining free allowance (Otter, Fuse) or "continue with free" (Mixpanel). Duolingo is the clearest case: free trial, currency, ad and free top-up all sit on one sheet, with the trial option visually loudest.
7. **Hard block at the moment of exhaustion (10 apps).** The task stops and the screen becomes the upsell: Duolingo out of hearts mid-lesson, Lex daily cap, Forest ad cap, Perplexity out of credits, X AI limit inline in chat, Bevel weekly limit, Riveo coin cost. Web: Krea, Emergent (inside the agent thread), PlayAI. AI apps tend to put the block inside the conversation or result area, not as a separate paywall.
8. **Trial or reverse-trial countdown inside the product (6 apps).** Duolingo (trial ends in N days, stay on Super), Headway (expiry date plus a "you'll lose" list and a discount), Monzo (a nav tab for the paid tier counting down a free week and showing what you've used), Atoms (days left, falls back to free with no auto-charge). Web: Klaviyo, Intercom. Monzo is the only clean reverse trial: everyone gets the paid tier for a week, and the tab reports usage of paid features.

Less common:
- **Blur or partial-result gates (5):** Tinder likes grid, Chime deals, Photoroom blurred example, Neuecast partial stats, Tolan blurred reading.
- **Time-limited in-product discounts (6):** Gentler Streak 47-hour countdown, Headway, Spotify $0-for-months, Strava + Runna bundle, Emergent +20% credits, Vivid price lock.
- **Upsell right after a success moment (3, weak signal):** Synthesia shows "time left" right after a video is created, MyFitnessPal says "your meal plan is ready" behind Premium+, and Future Pro asks for a referral after a workout. No clean "you just did X, go Pro" screen was found in consumer iOS apps.

## 3. Category notes

- **Education (Duolingo, Headway, Mimo, Insight Timer as content).** Gamified scarcity (hearts/energy) with several escape routes. The free-trial option is visually dominant and preselected. Tier ladder (Super → Max → family) for payers. Trial-ending screens use loss lists. AI tutoring features are the lever for the new top tier (Duolingo Max, Mimo credits).
- **Health & Fitness (MyFitnessPal, Strava, Cal AI, Calm, Bevel, Ahead, Gentler Streak, WHOOP, Insight Timer).** Few hard walls in daily logging. Upsells live in persistent surfaces (crown row, settings card) and in tier/family/bundle offers to payers. AI coach apps (Bevel, Ahead) add a daily or weekly allowance plus credit packs on top of the subscription. Family plans are common (Calm, Cal AI).
- **Productivity (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Notion, Todoist, Atoms, Raycast, Opal, Forest, web tools).** AI usage is metered by messages or credits, shown as a bar in settings. Limits show up inline where the work happens (chat thread, agent run). Team/permission features are gated with a "do it on the web" handoff (Todoist). Multi-tier pickers with a cheap entry tier (ChatGPT Go) appear.
- **Utilities (Google One, GoPro cloud, iCloud+ via Apple Invites).** Storage meters with a cheapest-tier card. Google puts a free "clean up" action before the paid one. Annual-switch banners for monthly payers.
- **Photo & Video (CapCut, Photoroom, Canva, Riveo, GoPro Quik, Krea web).** Heaviest use of credits and packs, counters next to the generate button, top-tier gates on video generation, and blurred example previews. Storage is sold as a separate subscription (CapCut Cloud).
- **Media & Entertainment (Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Suno, Neuecast).** Permanent upsell real estate (Premium tab, home banner). Long $0 intro offers, sometimes routed to web checkout (Spotify). Family plan offers to individual subscribers. Few in-flow walls in the screens found.
- **Social & Lifestyle (Tinder, Coffee Meets Bagel, Lex, Yubo, Azar, Replika, X, Apple Invites, Recime).** The densest mix: blurred "who likes you" grids, daily caps, consumables with volume pricing, a currency as an alternative to subscribing, multi-tier ladders (Plus/Gold/Platinum, Plus/Premium/Supreme), and settings that list every tier and every consumable.
- **Business (Otter, Universe, Klaviyo, Mixpanel, Synthesia, Rox).** Quota-based soft gates that still let the action finish (Otter), empty-state upgrade CTAs on locked tabs (Universe), and on web, value-echo modals plus usage dashboards with confirm-upgrade dialogs that state when billing starts.

## 4. Gaps: what we couldn't find

- **Grammarly, Lensa, Remini, Picsart, VSCO, Evernote, Dropbox, Shazam, YouTube Music free tier, Headspace locked content, Cal AI scan-limit screens.** Searches returned generic paywalls or nothing in-product for these apps. Spotify returned only the Premium tab and post-purchase screens: no skip-limit or shuffle-only wall.
- **Notion AI limit on iOS.** Only a credit-usage sheet and a feature-tour coach mark. No "AI responses used up" wall.
- **ChatGPT free-limit trigger.** Only the plan picker. The screen that fires when the free model limit is hit wasn't found.
- **A clean "success moment → upgrade" screen in consumer iOS apps.** Post-workout and post-lesson screens found were celebration or referral screens.
- **Frequency or capping logic.** Mobbin can't show how often a prompt reappears, whether it's capped per session, or what happens on the second dismiss.
- **Monthly→annual switch prompts in subscription-first consumer apps.** Found only in Coinbase (finance) and as a GoPro banner. No Duolingo, Calm or Headspace in-app "switch to annual" screen.
- **Downgrade or cross-grade flows started by the app** (other than WHOOP showing downgrade beside upgrade).
- **Ad-supported "watch an ad to unlock a premium feature"** in subscription apps beyond Duolingo energy and Forest. Other ad-reward hits were rewards/loyalty apps (Sweatcoin, TextNow, Discord quests).
- **Android.** The MCP covers iOS and web only.
