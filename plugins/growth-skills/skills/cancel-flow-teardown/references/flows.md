Reviewed 2026-10-29 with the Mobbin MCP (`search_flows`, iOS unless marked web). Screens are described in our own words; no Mobbin text or images are copied. Open the `mobbin_url` to see them. "Screens" is the length of the flow as Mobbin recorded it; the search returns evenly spaced previews, so a few middle screens per flow were not seen, and details marked "not seen" may exist. Mobbin captures are dated by the app version, not by us, so a flow may have changed since.

Bucket = RevenueCat SOSA category (primary App Store category). "Store hand-off" = the flow ends in Apple's subscription sheet / Settings rather than cancelling in the app.

## Patterns (22 subscription apps above)

**Entry point.** All have it under Settings / Account / Profile. Labels vary: "Manage subscription" (most), "Subscription details: Edit" (Headspace), "Memberships" (Peloton), a red "Cancel" row at the bottom of a benefits page (Deepstash, Blinkist), a status row "Pro — Active" (Tide Guide). The strongest entry points show plan, price and renewal date before any cancel action (Spotify, YouTube, Strava, MyFitnessPal, Dave, Grammarly).

**Depth before the store hand-off.** Three shapes:
1. **Straight to the store** (0 own screens): Babbel, Calm (text link), Audible (text only, no button).
2. **One or two interstitials** (survey and/or one save screen): Lifesum, MyFitnessPal, Centr, Finimize, Grammarly, Glovo, YouTube.
3. **Longer sequences** (3+ own screens before the hand-off): Duolingo (plan switch, loss, then store), Deepstash (open-text survey, loss screen, instructions), Strava (benefits recap, survey), Headway (long survey), SiriusXM (long survey).

**Offer types seen.** Plan switch / downgrade (Duolingo, Peloton), discount (Pillow 25% off annual for a year with a countdown; Plum 50% off for 6 months), loss reminder with no offer (Duolingo, Deepstash, Strava, Finimize, Grammarly, Centr, Blinkist), social loss (Blinkist shared account), upsell to a bigger plan on the manage page (Calm family, YouTube family, Tide Guide lifetime/family, Audible annual). Only one discount per flow; no stacked offers seen.

**Pause.** Only Dave (own billing) offers a billing pause. No App Store app shows one, which matches Lazyweb's 0/48 (#C27) and the fact that iOS has no store pause. Strava lists "temporarily pausing" as a survey reason without offering a pause: a missed match between reason and offer.

**Survey.** 12 of 22 have one (Duolingo and Blinkist only by Mobbin's "Giving Feedback" tag). Lengths 3–11 reasons. Only SiriusXM (Skip in header), Plum (Skip) and Headspace ("if you don't mind sharing") make it visibly optional. Centr and Lifesum appear to require an answer to continue. Mandatory surveys were cited in FTC v. Chegg, and UK draft guidance says feedback requests must be optional.

**Button hierarchy.** Two schools. "Keep" as primary and cancel as text link: Duolingo, Deepstash, Blinkist, Glovo, Peloton. "Cancel" as primary (filled) and "keep" as secondary: Centr ("cancel in settings" in red), Grammarly, Strava (survey screen: keep outlined, cancel filled grey). The second is closer to the "as easy as signing up" principle.

**Store hand-off quality.** Best: open Apple's sheet in-app (Babbel, Headway end on Apple's Edit Subscription sheet, which suggests `showManageSubscriptions`). Weaker: text instructions with a Settings path and no link (Finimize, Audible, Deepstash's "see how to cancel"). The text-only version adds steps and can go stale as iOS menus change.

**Confirmation and after cancel.** Good examples show end-of-access date and no further charge (Centr, Grammarly with date and time, Givingli, Strava, Headway, Pillow, Blinkist "you will not be charged"). Re-subscribe is one tap from the same card (Strava, Pillow, Grammarly, Blinkist "Reactivate"). Headway and Plum show a gift/upgrade nudge right after. Apple's own sheet shows "cancelled, ends on…" with a Renew button, so apps that hand off get a confirmation for free.

**Personalisation.** Name in the headline (Centr, Grammarly), personal usage stats (Finimize paid articles read; Glovo savings per order), tenure ("subscriber since…", Strava).
